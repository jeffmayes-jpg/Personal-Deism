We Are Here to Help Others Flourish

Because every person possesses intrinsic worth and personal flourishing is sacred, persons have a responsibility to contribute, when reasonably able, to the flourishing of others.


Helping others flourish does not mean imposing our beliefs upon others. Each person is responsible for themselves. This means that each person is likewise free to choose what beliefs they hold.


Question

Am I obligated to help others flourish?


Response

Personal Deism does not coerce adherence. However, it remains a tenet. Whether or not to adhere to a tenet is a choice left to the individual, as are the consequences of that choice.
