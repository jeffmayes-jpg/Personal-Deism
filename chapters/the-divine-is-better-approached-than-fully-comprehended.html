The Divine Is Better Approached Than Fully Comprehended


Human language and concepts can point toward ultimate reality but cannot exhaustively describe it. Humility is therefore an intellectual virtue.


The deific can be experienced, but human language cannot adequately describe /exhaustively describe/ it.


Objection

If the divine cannot be fully described, meaningful discussion is impossible.


Response

Incomplete understanding does not preclude meaningful understanding. Science likewise advances through partial yet increasingly accurate models. Human language can point toward the deific without exhausting it.


Question

What does Personal Deism claim to know about the divine?


Response

The divine is capable of being related to personally and communally. Each individual may encounter and understand the divine based upon their own capabilities, experiences, and circumstances. Likewise, communities may develop shared understandings of the divine. Each individual thrives or not based upon expression of the values listed herein. Likewise, a society. Neither individual nor communal interpretation is presumed infallible.


Question

What does Personal Deism regard as ultimately unknowable about the divine?


Response

Human language is not able to fully express the divine. That does not preclude knowing and acting.
