Unity Within Diversity


Differences enrich civilization without negating the shared dignity and interconnectedness of all people.


Distinctions of any kind do not diminish intrinsic dignity. The shared dignity and interconnectedness of all persons remains.


Objection

Differences obviously matter.


Response

Differences enrich civilization and should not be ignored. The tenet claims only that such differences do not negate shared dignity. Differences may nevertheless be morally relevant when determining needs, responsibilities, rights in particular circumstances, or appropriate forms of treatment, provided they are not used to rank intrinsic worth. Unity does not require uniformity. Distinct persons contribute different abilities, perspectives, and experiences while remaining equal in dignity.
