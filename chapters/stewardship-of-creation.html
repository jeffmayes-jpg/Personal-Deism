Stewardship of Creation


Humanity bears responsibility for preserving and improving the natural world rather than exploiting it without regard for future generations. Likewise, persons bear responsibility for husbanding their environment.


It is an inherent responsibility of humanity to husband the earth. Here, husbanding means to aid in thriving, not rule over.


Objection

Humanity has the right to exploit nature.


Response

Humanity's unique abilities imply corresponding responsibilities. Stewardship seeks the flourishing of creation rather than domination over it. The claim is often that humanity's superior intelligence or productive needs justify unrestricted use of nature. Personal Deism rejects this conclusion. Greater capability creates greater responsibility rather than greater license.


No person possesses authority merely by being more capable, more intelligent, wealthier, more virtuous, or more influential. Legitimate authority exists only when justified by the rights, legitimate interests, consent, fiduciary responsibility, or lawful institutional role relevant to the circumstances.
