Freedom and responsibility are inseparable


Individuals possess genuine agency and are accountable for the consequences of their choices.

Accountability for one’s actions is an inherent part of being a person.


Objection

Freedom means doing whatever one wishes.


Response

Freedom without responsibility undermines the equal freedom of others. Genuine liberty requires accountability. Because every person possesses equal basic freedom, no person's liberty includes an unlimited authority to deprive another person of equivalent liberty.
