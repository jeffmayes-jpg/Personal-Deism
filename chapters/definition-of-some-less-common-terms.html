Definition of some less common terms


A lack of agency is the feeling or state of having no control over your life, choices, or actions. It means feeling like a passenger who reacts to events instead of an active driver who shapes them.


Epistemology is the branch of philosophy that studies knowledge. It asks what knowledge is, how we get it, and what limits our understanding. People often call it the "theory of knowledge". It tries to figure out how we tell the difference between what is true and what is just a guess or a belief.


The word teleological is an adjective that describes the idea or belief that things—like nature, actions, or the universe—have a clear purpose, goal, or final design rather than just happening by random chance.
