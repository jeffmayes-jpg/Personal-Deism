Conflict Is Not an End in Itself


While struggle may contribute to growth, unnecessary conflict should be reduced. Cooperation is viewed as a higher expression of personal potential.


What conflict would be considered necessary? Protection of another person's freedom or physical safety when no less restrictive means is reasonably capable of doing so.


Objection

History shows conflict drives progress.


Response

Progress often results from responding to conflict, not from conflict itself. Personal Deism values constructive struggle while treating violence as a last resort.


Objection

Pacifism is unrealistic.


Response

The philosophy is not absolute pacifism. It permits force when necessary to protect another person's freedom or physical safety and when no less restrictive means is reasonably available.
