Summary


A. Foundational metaphysics

The divine exists, and is good.

Reality is panentheistic, as reasoned.

Persons participate in the divine, per conviction. This is a metaphysical commitment of the system rather than an empirically established proposition.

Moral Growth Is the objective purpose of personal existence


B. Foundational anthropology

Persons have dignity.

Capability varies independently of dignity.


C. Ethics

Intrinsic value generates moral reasons.

Freedom generates agency.

Capability generates responsibility.

Rights limit legitimate authority.

Virtue constitutes moral growth.


D. Political philosophy

Equal dignity entails equal basic rights.

Government exists to protect legitimate rights rather than enforce virtue generally.

Moral obligations exceed legal obligations.


E. Epistemology

Truth is worth pursuing.

Reason and spiritual insight are complementary.

Empirical claims remain answerable to evidence.

Human knowledge remains incomplete.


F. Social philosophy

Cooperation is preferable to unnecessary conflict.

Diversity does not negate equality.

Persons have stewardship responsibilities.

Personal flourishing is sacred.
